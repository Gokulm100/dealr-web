Delete your Dealr account

Developer: Dealr · Contact: support@dealrapp.in

Use the steps below to request that your Dealr account and associated personal data are deleted. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email support@dealrapp.in from the same Google account email you use to sign in to Dealr.
  2. Use the subject line: Dealr Account Deletion Request
  3. In the email body, include:
    • Your full name as shown in Dealr
    • The email address linked to your Google Sign-In
    • A clear request to delete your Dealr account and associated data
  4. We will verify the request and delete your account within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email when it is complete.

Data that is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your profile details (name, email, profile photo, phone number if provided)
  • Your active and past ads / listings and uploaded images
  • Your chat messages and conversation history
  • Favourites, reviews you submitted, and in-app preferences
  • Device tokens used for push notifications
  • Consent and account authentication records tied to your user ID

Data that may be retained

Some information may be kept for a limited time where required for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance:

  • Abuse / safety reports and moderation logs — retained up to 90 days after deletion, then removed or anonymised
  • Aggregated analytics that no longer identify you (for example, counts of views) may be kept
  • Information we must keep under applicable law (for example, if required for an ongoing legal matter)

After the retention period ends, retained personal data is deleted or anonymised.

Additional notes

  • Deleting your Dealr account does not delete your Google account.
  • If you only want to stop AI-related processing, you can withdraw consent in the app under Profile → Privacy & Terms without deleting your account.
  • Privacy policy: https://dealrapp.in/privacy
