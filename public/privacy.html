Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 July 2026 · Policy version v1 · Contact: support@dealrapp.in

We prioritize your privacy. Your data is handled in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act, India).

Privacy Notice

  1. What Data We Collect
    We may collect and process the following when you use our app:
    • Ad content (title, description, images, price, category, location)
    • Chat messages exchanged with buyers/sellers
    • Device information and usage activity (views, clicks, search patterns)
    • Approximate location shared through your ad or device (with permission)
    • Profile details (name, phone number, email)
  2. Why We Collect This Data
    We use your data to:
    • Provide AI-based insights, recommendations, and improved ad visibility
    • Analyse ad performance and user behaviour
    • Detect fraud, spam, and unsafe or illegal activity
    • Personalize your user experience
    • Improve app quality, safety, and performance
    • Provide customer support when needed
  3. How We Use AI
    Our AI systems may:
    • Analyse your chat content to detect scams or risky conversations
    • Summarise ad behaviour to give you insights
    • Predict buyer interest and improve search matching
    • Flag policy violations or harmful behaviour
    We do not sell your data to advertisers or third parties.
  4. Your Rights Under DPDP Act (India)
    You have the right to:
    • Give or withdraw consent at any time
    • Access your personal data
    • Correct inaccurate data
    • Ask for data erasure (unless required by law)
    • Request a copy of your data
    • Raise concerns with our Data Protection Officer (DPO)
  5. Data Sharing
    We may share data only with:
    • Service providers who help us operate the app
    • Law enforcement, if legally required
    • Analytics partners (without personally identifying you)
    We will never sell your data.
  6. Data Storage & Protection
    Your data is encrypted and stored securely.
    Access is restricted only to authorised personnel.
    Data is retained only as long as necessary for service purposes or legal requirements.
  7. Withdrawal of Consent
    You can withdraw consent anytime under Settings → Privacy & Terms in the Dealr app or website.
    Withdrawing consent will not block access to the app, but AI-based features will stop working.
  8. Contact
    For privacy questions or data requests, email support@dealrapp.in.
